Pregnancy Journey

Understanding Your Trimesters

Explore key milestones and maternal health focus points across the 40 weeks.

First Trimester: The Foundation

The first 13 weeks are crucial for embryonic growth, organ development, and adjusting to major hormonal shifts.

  • ✓ Fetal heartbeat develops around weeks 5–6
  • ✓ Essential daily folic acid intake & hydration
  • ✓ Managing nausea, morning sickness & fatigue
💡 Janani Tip

"Small, frequent meals and gentle rest can significantly soothe morning sickness. Avoid self-medication during early organ development."

Second Trimester: The Golden Period

Weeks 14 to 26 often bring renewed energy as the baby grows rapidly, developing hearing, fingernails, and active kicks.

  • ✓ Feeling first baby kicks (quickening)
  • ✓ Anomaly ultrasound scans around week 18–20
  • ✓ Calcium, iron & nutrient-rich diet support
💡 Janani Tip

"Sleeping on your side (especially left) improves blood flow to your baby and eases back strain."

Third Trimester: Preparation & Bloom

From week 27 to delivery, your baby gains weight and lung maturity while you prepare for birth.

  • ✓ Tracking daily fetal movement & kick counts
  • ✓ Recognizing genuine labor vs. Braxton Hicks
  • ✓ Hospital bag & birth plan preparations
💡 Janani Tip

"Practice rhythmic breathing and gentle pelvic stretches. Contact your hospital immediately if you experience reduced baby movements."
