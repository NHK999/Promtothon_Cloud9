🥑 Trimester Nutrition Pillars

  • Iron & Folate: Dark leafy greens, lentils, beans, pomegranates for blood volume.
  • Calcium & Vit D: Curd, ragi, paneer, and sesame for baby's bone development.
  • DHA Omega-3: Walnuts, chia seeds, and fortified supplements for fetal brain growth.
  • Gentle Fiber: Whole grains and fruits to ease digestion and soothe constipation.

🧘 Safe Daily Stretches

  • Cat-Cow Stretch: Relieves lower back pressure and eases pelvic stiffness.
  • Butterfly Pose (Baddha Konasana): Opens hip flexors and supports pelvic mobility.
  • Left-Side Resting: Optimizes oxygenated blood circulation to the placenta.
  • Pelvic Floor Breaths: Gentle diaphragmatic breathing to prepare for birth.
